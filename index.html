NES + is a system I devised to honor the NES console.

It is the bridge between the 8bit NES system, and the 16 bit SNES system.

Taking the limitations of the NES, and doubling their capabilities, while still being true to the system.

To give a modernized style of video game(s)
The realm quiz is essential to being sorted into your Ardron Realm.

You will answer 33 questions, while the arbiter watches over your

and judges your fate, based on your choices.
A fun quiz mostly created due to how Daemon Protocol functions.

You will answer a series of questions based on the six stats of Ardron.

Strength, Dexterity, Vitality, Intelligence, Wisdom, and Psyche.

Through your choices, these stats will be rated between -100 to +100.
