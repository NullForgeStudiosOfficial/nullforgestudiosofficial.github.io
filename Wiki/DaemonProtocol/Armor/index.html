Armor Information
Armor is equipment that provides defensive bonuses to your character. Each armor has a base set of values distributed across four defensive attributes: Evasion, Defense, Ward, and Impedance. Armor is divided into three classifications: Light Armor: Focuses on a single defensive attribute, primarily Evasion, or Ward. Medium Armor: Distributes its defensive bonuses across multiple attributes, allowing for more balanced defensive builds. Heavy Armor: Focuses on a single defensive attribute, primarily Defense or Impedance.
Armor Protections
Armor provides four types of protection against incoming attacks: • Evasion: Chance to avoid physical attacks. • Ward: Chance to avoid or protect against magical attacks. • Defense: Flat reduction to physical damage. (e.g. 50 damage - 45 defense = 5 damage. ) • Impedance: Flat reduction to magical damage.
Armor Upgrades
Armor may be upgraded if you have the correct materials to do so, and you know a friend with a high enough Blacksmithing Trade tier. Armor may be upgraded up to 6 times. Each upgrade increases the armor's protection values by 15%, compounded from the previous level. Only the displayed values are rounded down. (e.g. Gambeson Level 0 increased to Gambeson Level 1 goes from 45 Evasion to 51 Evasion.) The higher the tier an armor is, the more difficult it is to upgrade it. If an Armor has Modifiers, it also becomes more difficult to upgrade it.
Extra Attributes
Armor may also possess positive and negative modifiers that alter its properties. Positive modifiers can improve defensive attributes, increase other character statistics, or provide resistance to specific damage types. Negative modifiers can reduce character statistics or introduce weaknesses to specific damage types. See the Armor List for individual armor statistics, or Modifiers for a complete list of available equipment modifiers.

Armor Modifiers
Armor like weapons can possess up to 2 Modifiers. They can be either positive or negative in nature. A Positive Modifier's level is dependant on the Armors Rarity. Negative Modifiers can always be 0-6 regardless of the Armors Rarity.
