Bestiary Information
The Bestiary contains information about the various creatures and enemies found throughout Ardron. Each entry provides information about a creature's attributes, abilities, and other characteristics that may be useful when encountering it. Creatures are organized by their respective regions, allowing you to explore the inhabitants of Halycyon, Vector, Meridian, Praxis, and Echelon. Some creatures may be relatively harmless, while others can pose a significant threat to even experienced adventurers. Knowing what you're up against can mean the difference between victory and a very unfortunate encounter.
