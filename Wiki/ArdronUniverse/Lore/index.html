The Ardron Universe Lore Intro
Welcome to the Lore of Ardron Universe. Not every story will be told here. Many characters have their own tales to share, and some of those stories are best experienced through gameplay. Instead, this archive presents the history of Ardron Universe as told through the eyes of its six gods. Their experiences. Their perspectives. Their versions of the truth.
Informational Backgrounds
Throughout this archive, the background color will change to indicate which god is currently speaking. Red represents Iwata. Green represents Sythys. Black represents Entar. Blue represents Zykius. Silver represents Astus. White represents Tynos. Purple represents... Someone. Remember: A god's perspective is not necessarily the whole truth. Nor is their memory perfect. You have been warned.
Imperfect Memory
Some chapters are Unknown. Lost to time. The gods didn't record it, or the records were destroyed. These may be recovered with time...with the help of others. Who's to know.
