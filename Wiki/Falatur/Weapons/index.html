Weapons Overview
Weapons are the primary means of combat in Falatur. Every weapon must meet the following requirements before being used in combat: • Pass inspection by a Judge. • Be assigned a Primary Damage Type. • Be marked with the appropriate color band. Primary Damage Types Every weapon is assigned a Primary Damage Type during inspection, based on its construction and intended use. The Primary Damage Type determines which Damage Type properties and Weapon Properties apply to the weapon. Secondary Damage Types A weapon may also be assigned one Secondary Damage Type if its construction supports a distinct method of attack using that Damage Type. Secondary Damage Types may also be granted through Weapon Properties, abilities, or other effects. Secondary Damage Types do not benefit from special properties or bonuses associated with their Damage Type or method of attack. Only the weapon's Primary Damage Type may benefit from such properties unless a rule specifically states otherwise. A weapon may never possess more than two Damage Types simultaneously. Weapon Classification Weapons are classified according to their size, construction, and intended function. Weapon size determines its base damage, while its Weapon Family may grant additional properties or modify how the weapon functions. Weapons must reasonably resemble the weapons they are intended to represent in both appearance and function. Inspection and Approval Judges are responsible for determining a weapon's approved Damage Types and classification. A weapon may not be assigned a Damage Type simply because some part of its construction could theoretically produce that type of damage. For example, a Mace should not receive Slashing Damage merely because some portion of its construction could theoretically be used to cut. Once approved, a weapon's Primary or Secondary Damage Type may not be changed without reinspection by a Judge. Judges have final discretion when determining weapon classifications, Damage Types, and whether a weapon is appropriate for combat.
Damage Types
Weapons in Falatur use three different Damage Types: Piercing, Slashing, and Bludgeoning. Each Damage Type has its own properties and interactions with Armor, Shields, and limbs. A weapon's Primary Damage Type must be identified by a clearly visible color band. Piercing Damage Green Piercing weapons concentrate force into a single point, allowing attacks to bypass Armor and strike the target beneath. • Ignores Armor completely. • Deals no damage to Shields. • Cannot Sever or Break limbs. • Reduces limb Hit Points normally. Slashing Damage Red Slashing weapons use a defined cutting edge to deliver attacks. • Deals normal damage to Armor. • Deals no damage to Shields. • Can Sever limbs when reducing them to 0 Hit Points. Bludgeoning Damage Blue Bludgeoning weapons deliver force through impact rather than cutting or piercing. • Deals normal damage to Armor. • Deals damage to Shields. • Can Break limbs when reducing them to 0 Hit Points. Combined Damage Types Some weapons may possess both a Primary and Secondary Damage Type. A weapon may never possess more than two Damage Types simultaneously. When multiple Damage Types contribute to reducing a limb to 0 Hit Points, the Damage Type responsible for the final damage determines the resulting injury. If a single attack deals multiple Damage Types, only the weapon's Primary Damage Type determines the injury inflicted. Secondary Damage Types may still affect Armor, Shields, and other applicable mechanics normally. Damage Type Restrictions A weapon's Damage Types must reflect its approved construction and intended function. Secondary Damage Types do not receive special bonuses or properties associated with their Damage Type unless explicitly stated otherwise. Any changes to a weapon's approved Damage Types require reinspection by a Judge.
Weapon Lengths
Weapons in Falatur are divided into three size classifications: Small, Medium, and Large. Weapon size is determined by comparing the weapon's length to the body proportions of its wielder, rather than using fixed measurements. This allows weapon classifications to scale naturally with the size of each player. Small Weapons A Small weapon must be no longer than the distance from the wielder's hip to their knee. Weapons exceeding this length are considered Medium, provided they meet the requirements for that classification. Medium Weapons A Medium weapon must be longer than the distance from the wielder's hip to their knee, but no longer than the distance from the ground to their elbow. Medium weapons may be wielded with one or two hands, depending on their construction and intended function. Large Weapons A Large weapon must be longer than the distance from the ground to the wielder's shoulder. Large weapons must be wielded with two hands when performing attacks. Large weapons receive additional damage bonuses according to their Damage Type and Weapon Family. Unclassified Weapon Lengths Weapons exceeding the maximum length of Medium weapons, but failing to meet the minimum length of Large weapons, are not permitted in combat. This restriction maintains a clear distinction between Medium weapons and the significantly larger weapons capable of receiving additional damage bonuses. Compression Testing Weapon size is determined by its compression-tested length rather than its resting, uncompressed length. To compression test a weapon, place its striking tip against the ground and apply reasonable downward pressure to compress any safety padding. Compare the resulting length against the wielder's appropriate body measurement. Compressible safety padding does not increase a weapon's size classification beyond its compressed length. Non-compressible or minimally compressible components, such as EVA foam used for core protection, count toward the weapon's measured length. Pommel padding that does not contribute to the weapon's effective reach does not need to be included in its size classification. Length Restrictions Padding may not be excessively extended for the purpose of gaining additional reach or manipulating a weapon's size classification. Judges have final discretion when determining whether padding is reasonably constructed for safety or is being used to circumvent weapon-size restrictions. A weapon's classification is determined relative to its current wielder. A weapon approved for one player may fall into a different classification when used by another.
Weapon Damage
Weapons in Falatur deal damage according to their Size Classification and Primary Damage Type. Small, Medium, and Large weapons each have their own damage values. Certain weapons may also receive additional damage through Weapon Properties, abilities, or other effects. Small Weapon Damage • Slashing: 1 Slashing Damage. • Piercing: 1 Piercing Damage. • Bludgeoning: 2 Bludgeoning Damage. Small weapons receive no additional damage bonuses from their size. Medium Weapon Damage • Slashing: 2 Slashing Damage. • Piercing: 2 Piercing Damage. • Bludgeoning: 2 Bludgeoning Damage. Medium weapons receive no additional damage bonuses from their size. Large Weapon Damage • Slashing: 2 Slashing Damage + 2 Bludgeoning Damage. • Piercing: 2 Piercing Damage + 1 Bludgeoning Damage. • Bludgeoning: 2 Bludgeoning Damage + 2 Bludgeoning Damage. Large weapons receive additional Bludgeoning Damage according to their Primary Damage Type. These bonuses are applied alongside the weapon's normal damage, rather than replacing it. Weapon Properties Some Weapon Families possess special properties that modify their damage or grant additional methods of attack. These properties may increase damage, introduce Secondary Damage Types, or alter how a weapon functions. Weapon Properties take precedence over the standard damage values when explicitly stated. Combined Damage When an attack deals multiple Damage Types, only the Primary Damage Type determines the injury inflicted upon a limb. Secondary Damage Types may still affect Armor, Shields, and other applicable mechanics normally. For example, a Large Slashing weapon dealing 2 Slashing Damage and 2 Bludgeoning Damage may damage a Shield through its Bludgeoning component. However, when determining whether a limb is Severed or Broken, only the weapon's Primary Slashing Damage Type is considered. Damage Restrictions Weapons may not receive bonuses or special effects beyond those granted by their approved classification, Weapon Properties, abilities, or other applicable rules. A weapon's damage values must be approved during inspection and may not be changed without reinspection by a Judge.
Weapon Families
Weapons in Falatur are divided into different Weapon Families based on their construction, appearance, and intended function. While many weapons share similar dimensions, they may function very differently in combat. Each Weapon Family has its own requirements, Damage Types, and potentially unique Weapon Properties.
Daggers A Dagger is a Small weapon with a continuous rigid blade. Daggers deal 1 Piercing Damage. Daggers possess the Slashing property, allowing them to deal 1 Slashing Damage when performing a Slashing attack.
Rapiers A Rapier is a Medium weapon with a continuous rigid blade terminating in a distinct thrusting tip. Rapiers must possess an enclosed hand guard, such as a basket hilt, for identification. Rapiers deal 2 Piercing Damage. Rapiers cannot slash like normal, but they are still capable of drawcuts. Rapier drawcuts deal 1 Slashing damage.
Spears A Spear is a Large weapon consisting of a rigid shaft terminating in a distinct thrusting tip. Spears deal 2 Piercing Damage. Spears possess the Punch Through property, granting an additional 1 Bludgeoning Damage when wielded with two hands.
Swords A Sword is a weapon with a continuous rigid blade and an exposed handle no longer than one-third of its overall length. Swords deal 2 Slashing Damage. Large Swords possess the Momentum property, granting an additional 1 Bludgeoning Damage when wielded with two hands. Swords without a hilt that possess a stabbing tip may deal 1 Piercing Damage using that tip.
Axes An Axe is a weapon with a rigid axe head mounted to a haft. The axe head must not exceed one-third of the weapon's overall length. Axes deal 2 Slashing Damage. Large Axes possess the Momentum property, granting an additional 1 Bludgeoning Damage when wielded with two hands.
Clubs A Club is a weapon with a continuous rigid striking surface and no defined edge. The exposed handle must not exceed one-third of the weapon's overall length. Clubs deal 2 Bludgeoning Damage. Large Clubs possess the Momentum property, allowing them to deal 4 Bludgeoning Damage when wielded with two hands.
Hammers A Hammer is a weapon with a distinct rigid striking head mounted to a haft. The striking head must not exceed one-third of the weapon's overall length. Hammers deal 2 Bludgeoning Damage. Large Hammers possess the Momentum property, allowing them to deal 4 Bludgeoning Damage when wielded with two hands.
Flails A Flail is a weapon with a distinct striking head connected to a haft by a flexible tether. Flails must extend above the wielder's elbow, but must not reach the ground when measured from the wielder's elbow. The striking head must not exceed one-third of the weapon's overall length. Flails deal 2 Bludgeoning Damage. Flails possess no additional Weapon Properties.
Bows A Bow is a ranged weapon designed to launch arrows. Bows may not be used to perform melee attacks. A Bow must not exceed a draw weight of 30 pounds at a 30-inch draw. Arrows deal 2 Piercing Damage when fired from a Bow. Arrows may also be used as thrusting weapons, dealing 1 Piercing Damage. Arrows must weigh less than 16 ounces. Bows possess the Ammunition property and require an arrow to be nocked before firing.
Halberds A Halberd is a polearm with multiple functional striking surfaces mounted to a rigid haft. When the butt of the haft rests on the ground beside the wielder, the highest striking surface must reach at least the wielder's shoulder. Halberds must possess at least two distinct functional striking surfaces belonging to different weapon types. Each striking surface must be clearly distinguishable. Halberds deal 1 Damage of the appropriate Damage Type for the striking surface used. Halberds possess the Versatile property, allowing them to deal Slashing, Piercing, or Bludgeoning Damage depending on the striking surface used.
Javelins A Javelin is a ranged weapon with a rigid shaft terminating in a distinct thrusting tip. Javelins must weigh less than 16 ounces. Javelins deal 2 Piercing Damage when thrown. When used as thrusting weapons, Javelins deal 1 Piercing Damage. Javelins possess the Thrown property and must be thrown to deal their full damage.
Whips A Whip is a flexible weapon consisting of a handle connected to a flexible striking length. Whips must be constructed in 6-inch sections, with gaps no greater than 1 inch between sections. Whips deal 2 Bludgeoning Damage. Whips possess no additional Weapon Properties.
Chakrams A Chakram is a circular throwing weapon consisting of a complete ring with a padded striking edge. Chakrams deal 1 Slashing Damage when thrown. Chakrams may not be used as melee weapons or wielded like Daggers. Chakrams possess the Thrown property.
Unusual Weapons Weapons must reasonably resemble the Weapon Family they are intended to represent. Weapons with unusual designs are permitted, provided they meet all applicable safety and construction requirements. If a weapon does not clearly fit an existing Weapon Family, a Judge will determine its classification. Weapons may not be intentionally designed to confuse players or circumvent the rules.
Weapon Properties
Weapon Properties are special rules assigned to certain Weapon Families that modify how they function in combat. These properties may alter a weapon's Damage Types, increase its damage, or introduce additional methods of attack. Weapon Properties only apply when their specific requirements are met. Slashing The Slashing property allows certain weapons whose Primary Damage Type is Piercing to perform Slashing attacks. Daggers: May perform Slashing attacks dealing 1 Slashing Damage. Rapiers: May perform Draw Cuts dealing 1 Slashing Damage. Rapiers may not perform conventional Slashing attacks. Slashing attacks are subject to the normal rules for Slashing Damage, including the ability to Sever limbs. Momentum The Momentum property allows certain Large weapons to deal additional damage when wielded with two hands. The bonus depends on the weapon's family. • Large Swords: +1 Bludgeoning Damage. • Large Axes: +1 Bludgeoning Damage. • Large Clubs: Deal 4 Bludgeoning Damage instead of 2. • Large Hammers: Deal 4 Bludgeoning Damage instead of 2. Momentum does not apply to Medium or Small weapons. Punch Through Punch Through allows Spears to deal additional Bludgeoning Damage when wielded with two hands. A Spear using Punch Through deals its normal 2 Piercing Damage and an additional 1 Bludgeoning Damage. Versatile The Versatile property allows a weapon to deal different Damage Types depending on the striking surface used. Halberds: May deal Slashing, Piercing, or Bludgeoning Damage using their corresponding approved striking surfaces. Each successful attack deals 1 Damage of the appropriate type. Each striking surface must be clearly distinguishable and approved during inspection. Ammunition The Ammunition property applies to weapons that require a separate projectile to perform ranged attacks. Bows: Must have an arrow nocked before firing. Projectiles must meet all applicable construction and safety requirements. Thrown The Thrown property allows a weapon to be intentionally thrown at another player. Only weapons specifically designed and approved for throwing may use this property. Javelins: Deal 2 Piercing Damage when thrown, or 1 Piercing Damage when used as thrusting weapons. Chakrams: Deal 1 Slashing Damage when thrown and may not be used as melee weapons. Weapons Without Properties Some Weapon Families possess no additional properties. These weapons function according to their normal Damage Types, damage values, and applicable combat rules. Property Restrictions Weapon Properties may only be used by weapons that meet their requirements. A weapon may not benefit from properties associated with its Secondary Damage Type unless explicitly permitted by the applicable rule. Judges have final authority when determining whether a weapon qualifies for a particular property.
