Combat Overview
Falatur combat is built upon the Rule of Honor. Players are responsible for acknowledging valid hits, tracking their own injuries, and ensuring that combat remains safe and fair. Combat Basics All players begin with the same number of Hit Points. Each arm, each leg, and the torso have 2 Hit Points. Weapons deal damage according to their classification, with different damage types affecting players and their limbs in different ways. A hit is only valid when both players acknowledge that it was delivered with reasonable force and that it does not violate the rules governing illegal hits. Defeat A player is killed when their torso reaches 0 Hit Points, regardless of the condition of their limbs. A killed player must be Revived before returning to combat in a skirmish. Rule of Honor Players are expected to acknowledge valid hits honestly, respect their opponents, and prioritize safety over victory. When uncertainty arises, players should resolve disagreements respectfully or request a Judge. When in doubt, favor your opponent.
Hit Points
All players have the same number of Hit Points. Each arm, each leg, and the torso have 2 Hit Points. A player is killed when their torso is reduced to 0 Hit Points, regardless of the condition of their limbs. A killed player must be Revived before returning to play in a skirmish. If a limb at 0 Hit Points is struck again, the damage is transferred to the torso instead, unless the limb is Severed. Hit Locations The following are considered part of the Arm: • Hand • Forearm • Upper arm • Shoulder The following are considered part of the Leg: • Foot • Lower Leg • Thigh The following are considered part of the Torso: • Chest • Abdomen • Back • Hips • Underarm
Valid Hits
A slashing weapon may perform a draw cut by sliding its striking surface across a target with reasonable force. If a player catches or grabs the striking surface of a slashing weapon, the hand is considered hit and the weapon must immediately be released. A piercing attack may be delivered by either a thrust or a push. A push is the application of continuous forward pressure with a legal striking tip until both players acknowledge that sufficient force has been applied under the Rule of Honor. A hit is only valid if both players acknowledge that it was delivered with reasonable force, as described in the Rule of Honor, and it is not considered an Illegal Hit.
Illegal Hits A hit to a player's head or groin results in the attacker's immediate death, regardless of armor or remaining Hit Points. If the struck player believes the hit was accidental, they may waive this penalty under the Rule of Honor. No portion of a weapon may be used to protect a valid target. Any body part enclosed by a weapon remains a valid target, unless the enclosure is a natural part of the weapon's real-world design. (e.g. the basket hilt of a rapier.) Shields are exempt from this rule. A player may not use a weapon to cover a valid target in an attempt to prevent damage. If a weapon is positioned over a valid target, the covered body part is still considered struck. (e.g. laying the flat of a sword across the torso.) Shields are exempt from this rule. A weapon must strike a player directly to score a valid hit. Hits resulting solely from a weapon rebounding or deflecting off another weapon do not count.
Damage Types
Falatur recognizes three Damage Types: Piercing, Slashing, and Bludgeoning. Each Damage Type interacts differently with armor, shields, and limbs. A weapon may never possess more than two Damage Types at once. Piercing Damage Piercing weapons concentrate force into a single point, slipping past armor and striking the target beneath. • Ignores Armor completely. • Deals no damage to Shields. • Cannot Sever or Break limbs. • Damage dealt to a limb reduces its Hit Points normally. Once the limb reaches 0 Hit Points, no further effects occur. Slashing Damage Slashing weapons deal damage through their designated cutting surfaces. • Deals regular damage to Armor. • Deals no damage to Shields. • Slashing damage Severs limbs. A Severed limb may no longer be used. Bludgeoning Damage Bludgeoning weapons deal damage through blunt force. • Deals regular damage to Armor. • Deals damage to Shields. • Bludgeoning damage Breaks limbs. A Broken limb cannot be used to attack, block, or perform actions requiring normal use of that limb.
Weapon Handling
Weapons are the primary means of combat in Falatur. Weapons must be used according to their approved Damage Types and weapon properties. Grabbing Weapons If a player catches or grabs the striking surface of a Slashing weapon, the hand is considered hit and the weapon must immediately be released. Blocking With Weapons No portion of a weapon may be used to protect a valid target unless specifically permitted by the rules. A weapon positioned over a valid target does not prevent that body part from being struck. Shields are exempt from this restriction. Weapon Deflections A weapon must strike a player directly to score a valid hit. Hits resulting solely from a weapon rebounding or deflecting off another weapon do not count. Approved Weapon Functions A weapon's Primary Damage Type is determined during inspection based on its construction and intended use. A weapon may also possess one Secondary Damage Type if its construction or properties permit it. Secondary Damage Types do not benefit from special properties or bonuses associated with that damage type unless specifically stated otherwise. A weapon's approved Damage Types may not be changed without reinspection by a Judge. Draw Cuts Slashing weapons may perform a draw cut by sliding their designated striking surface across a valid target with reasonable force. A draw cut must maintain continuous contact with the target for a distance equal to at least half the weapon's length. A successful draw cut deals the weapon's normal Slashing Damage, as though the target had been struck by a regular attack. As with any other attack, both players must acknowledge that sufficient force was applied under the Rule of Honor.
Shields
Shields are handheld or arm-mounted pieces of equipment designed primarily to intercept and block incoming weapon attacks. A Shield must have a broad defensive surface distinct from a weapon's normal striking surfaces. A weapon's guard, basket, hilt, or other component intended primarily to protect the hand does not count as a Shield. Shield Hit Points All Shields have 4 Hit Points, regardless of their size. Only Bludgeoning Damage can reduce a Shield's Hit Points. Slashing and Piercing Damage deal no damage to Shields. When a Shield reaches 0 Hit Points, it is considered Broken and no longer provides protection. A Broken Shield may be repaired when its wielder is Revived, or when a Medic revives the equipment. Broken Shields A Broken Shield is treated as though it no longer exists for the purpose of blocking attacks. If a weapon strikes a Broken Shield, the attack continues through it and deals damage to any valid target behind it as normal. Players may continue carrying a Broken Shield, but it cannot be used to prevent incoming damage. Shields and Injured Limbs A Shield remains a valid target regardless of the condition of the limb carrying it. As long as the Shield physically intercepts an attack, its Hit Points are accounted for normally. This includes Shields carried by Broken or otherwise disabled limbs. Shield Restrictions Shields must meet the following minimum construction requirements: • At least 4 inches thick. • At least 12 inches wide. • Outside layers must be adequately padded. • All exposed edges and rims must be adequately padded. All Shields must pass equipment inspection before being used in combat. Shield Attacks A Shield may be used offensively, provided it has a clearly defined striking surface, such as a boss or point. The striking surface must be color-banded to indicate its approved Damage Type. A Shield approved for dealing damage has its maximum Hit Points reduced by 1, giving it a total of 3 Hit Points instead of 4. Simply having a boss, point, or other striking surface does not reduce a Shield's maximum Hit Points. The reduction only applies when the Shield is color-banded and approved as an attacking Shield. Shields without an approved, color-banded striking surface deal no damage. This applies regardless of whether the player performs a Shield bash, shove, or other offensive action using the Shield.
Injuries
In Falatur, limbs can become injured or disabled depending on the Damage Type of the attack that reduces their Hit Points. Each limb has 2 Hit Points. Limbs may be Healthy, Severed, Broken, or Dead. Healthy Limbs A Healthy limb is undamaged and functions normally. Limbs are restored to the Healthy state when the player is Revived. Severed Limbs Limbs are Severed by weapons dealing Slashing Damage. A Severed limb may no longer be used. If an arm is Severed, anything held by that arm must be dropped immediately. Players using strap Shields may simply allow the arm to hang, as attempting to remove the Shield during combat may result in death. If a leg is Severed, the player must balance on their remaining leg, hopping if necessary. A second successful hit to a Severed limb results in the player's death. Safety Exception Players may temporarily place weight on a Severed leg to maintain balance, prevent a fall, or otherwise avoid injury. Players may also land on a Severed foot when jumping, leaping, or recovering from movement. This exception is strictly for safety and does not permit the Severed leg to be used for normal movement, walking, running, or combat. Broken Limbs Limbs are Broken by weapons dealing Bludgeoning Damage. A Broken limb may hold a weapon, Shield, or item, but may not attack, block, or perform any action requiring normal use of that limb. Weapons and Shields held by a Broken limb may still receive incoming strikes normally. Dead Limbs A limb is considered Dead when it reaches 0 Hit Points for any reason. If a limb at 0 Hit Points is struck again, the damage is transferred to the torso instead, unless the limb is Severed. Piercing Damage cannot Sever or Break limbs. It reduces the limb's Hit Points normally, with no additional effects once the limb reaches 0 Hit Points. Combined Damage Types If a limb is reduced to 0 Hit Points by multiple Damage Types, the injury suffered is determined by the Damage Type that reduced the limb to 0 Hit Points. If an attack deals more than one Damage Type, only the weapon's Primary Damage Type determines injuries to limbs. Secondary Damage Types continue to affect Armor, Shields, and other applicable mechanics normally.
Death & Revival
Death A player is considered Dead when any of the following conditions are met: • Their Torso reaches 0 Hit Points. • Two of their limbs have been Severed. • Three of their limbs are Dead. When a player dies, they must immediately lie on the ground at the location of their death and remain there until Revived. In matches using Respawns, Dead players must instead return to their designated Respawn Point. Dead players may adjust their position slightly for comfort or safety, but may not intentionally obstruct living players. Dead players may call for a Medic, but may not otherwise communicate or participate in the battle. Revival Reviving a player restores all of their Hit Points, returns their limbs to the Healthy state, and fully repairs their Armor. Only designated Medics may revive other players. Any player may revive their own revivable equipment. To Revive a player or item, the person performing the Revival must maintain uninterrupted physical contact with both hands while audibly counting from "Revivify One" through "Revivify Ten." If physical contact is interrupted, or the person performing the Revival is struck, the Revival fails and the countdown must be restarted. Medics Medics are designated players capable of Reviving fallen allies. To qualify as a Medic, a player must meet the following requirements: • They may not wear Armor. • They must be approved by a Judge. • They must wear a clearly visible Medic insignia. A Medic automatically Revives 30 seconds after their own death.
Special Combat Rules
Controlled Force All players must fight using controlled force. Excessive force, reckless behavior, or intentionally attempting to injure another player is strictly prohibited. Regardless of the weapon being used, attacks must remain within the limits of the Rule of Honor. Blind Attacks Players may not intentionally strike targets they cannot reasonably see. Equipment may not be used to intentionally obstruct a player's vision. Physical Contact Intentional punching, kicking, elbowing, kneeing, grappling, tackling, and body-checking are prohibited unless explicitly permitted by the scenario. Incidental physical contact during normal combat is expected. Thrown Weapons Weapons may not be intentionally thrown unless they have been specifically designed and approved for throwing. Unsafe Conditions If a player notices an unsafe condition, they should immediately call "Hold!" All players must immediately stop fighting when "Hold!" is called. Combat may only resume once a Judge has declared the battlefield safe. Environmental Hazards Players must remain aware of their surroundings during combat. This includes hazards such as holes, uneven ground, rocks, low branches, spectators, and wildlife. A Judge may modify, suspend, or stop combat if the battlefield becomes unsafe. Equipment Safety All weapons, Shields, and other combat equipment must pass inspection before being used. Equipment that becomes unsafe during combat must be removed from the battlefield immediately. A Judge may inspect equipment at any time. Any significant modification to previously approved equipment requires reinspection before it may be used again. Judge's Discretion Judges have final authority over combat safety and rule enforcement. Any action or piece of equipment deemed unsafe may be prohibited, even if it technically complies with the written rules. Players may not intentionally exploit ambiguities or loopholes in the rules to gain an advantage. The Judge's decision is final. Safety Above Victory Players are expected to prioritize their own safety and the safety of others above winning. If a situation feels unsafe, stop combat and consult a Judge. No rule, objective, or victory is more important than a player's safety.
