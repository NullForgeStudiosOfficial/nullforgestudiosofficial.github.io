Foam lesson time!
structual foam is usually harder to squish. this is measured with IFD (Indentation Force Deflection), and LBF (Pounds of Force). the higher the IFD, the more pounds of force it takes to squish the foam. mk? mk. (notreallybutcloseenough) so if something has a 25% IFD of 30. that means it takes 30 pounds of force to squish the foam by 25% of its thickness. under specific testing conditions n shit. so 1 inch of foam becomes 0.75 inches. density is a different thing. thats basically how much the foam weighs for its size. higher density doesn't ALWAYS mean harder to squish. Hand Measurements
After you decided your length (🥵) You are next closing your hand into a fist... measuring it from one side to the next. Which is on average about 4 inches. You're gonna want some extra wiggleroom. so lets add +1 inch to w/e your measurement is. If you're making a dagger? 5 inches is all you need. If you're making a long sword, you can get away with a 5-8 inch handle. for a hand and a half sword type deal. if you're making a great sword. i'd recommend about 13 inches minimum, all that depending on your original hand measurement.
The Sandwich™
Now onto a building technique i call "The Sandwhich" You grab your handle material foam. either the BCM or EVA or whatever the hell you're using. and you cut off a length of foam, equal to the length of your hand measurement. Lets say 5 inches. and you make the thickness equal to your Fiberglass Rods thickness. lets say 1/2. so. a 5 inch x 1/2 piece. and then you do another piece just like that. and then... You DAP up those pieces. lay your fiberglass flat on your table or w/e. slap those 2 pieces on the sides of your fiberglass rod. Clamp it in place. Congrats. Now we wait an hour for it to semi-dry. You've begun "The Sandwhich™" Image for reference.
The Sandwich™ Part 2 Electric Boogaloo
NOW with that kinda firmly in place. (DAP takes 48 hours to cure completely, but we can continue working. just... be careful/gingerly) NOW. If you're using the BCM it is 0.5 inches thick. That means. 0.5inch BCM + 0.5 FGR + 0.5" BCM = 1.5 inches total. so. Now go ahead and cut a 5 inch x 1.5 inch piece of foam. .... and then do another. and yup. you guessed it. DAP and sandwhich that mf.
IF. you are using 6mm EVA...this changes however. 6mm EVA is closer to 0.25 inches. so 0.25" EVA + 0.5 FGR + 0.25" EVA = 1 inch. so do a 5x1 inch piece of material on each side. this is a thinner handle...and it makes the edge alignment wayyyy easier so you don't flat someone with the blade. This is why I make handles out of 6mm EVA. using BCM or 12mm eva all over. makes... a square. 6mm EVA on the sides makes this type of sandwich.
The Sandwich™ The Final Frontier
So you got your handle sandwhich. Time for the rest of the blade. ...yup. You just... sandwhich the whole ass fireglass rod now. BUT WITH AN IMPORTANT DETAIL. whatever your handle orientation is, you need to rotate it 90 degree's for your blade. so your handle edge feel, and the edge of the sword line up.
^ If this is your handle. V This needs to be your "Blade" construction.
The long sides (the 1.5" piece) will be where our strike foam goes. anyways. You want equal pieces. you WANT the square for the blade. ...thats it lmao. Sandwhich the blade. using the previous method. then... wait a couple hours. cause you jsut glued a whole bunch if you did it all at once. you're going to want this thing pretty set before you start the next part.
Endcap + Pommel
So. Now we have to make sure the core don't jump out of the grip of the foam holding onto its circular edges. yeah. that sentence is why we do this part. Endcap. mmk. i assume your sword end (the end WITHOUT the handle) is... relatively flat. What you're going to want to do. DAP the top of the blade where the rod is showing. Put a washer on the end. centered with the fiberglass rod (which is why i like washers, theres a hole. you can center it) and then cut a piece of your structural foam to match the dimensions of your "blade" If you're using 0.5 thick foam. that means you need a 1.5" x 1.5" square I would REALLY recommend 12mm EVA for this instead of the BCM and then lay that on top of the washer and the rest of the blade. NOW. If you bought the cotton tape? Feel free to tape that mf down. preferably on the "flat" of the blade. That's all for the endcap really...
Now...the pommel will be a little bit of a bitch. Due to ...safty reasons. The pommel will need to be 2.5 inches diameter. Now. Go cut a piece of whatever 0.5 inch foam you want. But make it a 2.5 inch circle. use a compass, a piece of string. a cardboard template (thats what i do) idgaf. just 2.5 inch circle. now. cut ANOTHER 2.5 inch circle........and another one. Three pieces total. ... Now the bitch part. ... center your handle on one of the pieces of foam. mark around your handle. now. cut that shape out of the one circle. having fun yet? good. NOW. dap the inside of that mf. ...and slide it on so its flush with the end of your handle. this is why we gave an extra inch of space. you've just created the platform for your pommel. NOW. Put a washer on the bottom of this platform/your handle. center that thing obviously. DAP the entire platform and washer. Lay your 2nd circle down on top of all that. ... Dap the top of the 2nd circle you just layed down. and then lay down your 3rd circle on top of that. It's recommended to use the BCM for this 🤷 i do. well. i use a puck of 12mm eva. and a puck of BCM. but whatever. anyways. NOW. you can use the tape if you bought some. you basically want to make an asterisk of tape. just * all 8 corners and edges. and then when you're done that. just... wrap around the bottom of this. I use this time to wrap my entire handle so it feels better. BUT. now we got safty, and security, and you got a bitchin handle. if you had the tape, you don't have to worry about waiting for the DAP to dry. tap ekeeps it in place. DAP makes it more secure so things dont slide around. Double duty the pommel. don't fuck around with pommel safty. a couple pennies of tape and DAP are a hell of a lot cheaper than paying for someones medical bills. Trust.
Finishing Touches
NOW onto the final part. The edge. striking surface. you're gonna whip out that upholstory foam. or whatever you're using. cut a 1.5 inch wide piece. oh. the length? Depends how you want to do it. There is 2 methods. 1. Length x2 + 6. Basically. if you have a 30 inch sword. You cut a 1.5 inch x 65 piece of upholstory foam piece. you then start from your handles end. go up...and around the end cap. and then follow through to the other side. 2. Cut two 1.5 pieces of foam equal to your weapons length. you then later cut a curved U shape of upholstory foam and attach it to the top for a thrusting tip. Whichever you prefer honestly. i prefer method 1. iwill warn you. that upholstory foam. (or any open cell foam really. as you should be using open cell for a striking surface...) is going to drink DAP like a fuckin frat boy. So yeah. be warned there. BUT let that all sit and cure for 48 hours. Give it a cloth wrapping somehow. ...and your sword is done.
