Foam lesson time!
structual foam is usually harder to squish. this is measured with IFD (Indentation Force Deflection), and LBF (Pounds of Force). the higher the IFD, the more pounds of force it takes to squish the foam. mk? mk. (notreallybutcloseenough) so if something has a 25% IFD of 30. that means it takes 30 pounds of force to squish the foam by 25% of its thickness. under specific testing conditions n shit. so 1 inch of foam becomes 0.75 inches. density is a different thing. thats basically how much the foam weighs for its size. higher density doesn't ALWAYS mean harder to squish.
