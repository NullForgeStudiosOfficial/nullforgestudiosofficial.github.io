The NES?
The NES is the Nintendo Entertainment System, a game console released by Nintendo in the 1980s. And compared to modern hardware, this thing is a fucking potato. It had an 8-bit CPU, about 2 KB of general-purpose RAM, extremely limited graphics hardware, and game cartridges that were often measured in kilobytes... not gigabytes. Developers couldn't just make whatever they wanted and throw more hardware at the problem. Every sprite, tile, color, sound, and byte of memory mattered. Those limitations are important, because NES+ starts with the question: “What if I made Ardron Universe games while deliberately obeying limitations similar to an NES?” Except... The + is where I start cheating.
NES Limitations
If the information above didn't give you an idea of its limitations... The average modern gaming PC, has enough RAM to hold the working memory of millions of NES consoles simultaneously. In raw clock speed alone, a modern multicore CPU operates on the scale of tens of thousands of NES CPU clocks at once and that's before accounting for the fact that modern CPUs accomplish vastly more per clock. So... This is FamiStudio. If you peek into the top left. There is. • Square 1 • Square 2 • Triangle • Noise • DPCM That's it. You get 5 "instruments" 🤨 Square 1 and 2 are basically the melody/lead Triangle is the "bass". as its the lowest sounding note. so 🤷 And noise is usually used for Drums...snares and hihats and such. DPCM is...fuckin confusing. It takes a sound sample and can play it back at different rates, which changes the pitch basically. idk. i don't fuck with it. You cannot really do chords with FamiStudio... Well. You can. But it comes at a cost. If you try to make 2 notes take up the same space in the same "instrument", one will just overwrite the other. So if you want 2 notes playing at the same time, you need to use 2 different "instruments". You could use Square 1 for one note and Square 2 for another to make a chord... But congratulations. You just used 2 of your 5 "instruments" to make ONE chord. 🤨 So most games use Square 1 and Square 2 for different parts of the song instead, and get creative when they want something to sound like a chord.
