The Black Dahlia Murder to me is... Well a savior of sorts --- To spare some tragedies and sympathy finding. Events happened. And instead of going the bad route. I had a period of about...5 months. where: 1. I woke up 2. I played BDM on guitar or drums. 3. I went to sleep. Yup. that was about it for 5 months straight as I worked through some things. So they saved me, cliche I know, but the lead singer, and band creator Trevor Strnad also delt with these problems. How much I don't know. On May 12, 2022. He lost that battle. I have not. not yet. Both Ardron Universe, and BDM has saved me. This is less than what they deserve. , but it's just a tribute. AU is dedicated to BDM. As without them, It would never be created, nor would I still be here. Show some love, and support at: Buy Merch Buy Music Buy Vinyls Don't have money? That's okay. Support doesn't have to cost a damn thing. Listen to their music, share it with someone, and help keep the metal alive. 🤘 Like it did me. RIP Trevor.
